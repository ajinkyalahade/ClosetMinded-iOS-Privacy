Everything your wardrobe needs, on one rail

Closet, Butler, Outfits, Trips and Laundry. Each one works from the same clothes, so you only ever add something once.

  • Butler

    Take a photo and it fills in the category, colour, formality and a name. A flat-lay of several pieces becomes several items.

  • Outfits

    Pick where you're going and get looks put together from your own clothes, with the missing piece named when there's a gap.

  • Trips

    Add a destination and dates. The packing list comes from your wardrobe and the daily forecast, up to 16 days out.

  • Laundry

    Mark things dirty as you wear them. They're sorted into loads you can actually run together.

What it changes

No promises about minutes saved. Here's what the app actually does, and where that helps.

Less time deciding what to wear

  • Outfits for the occasion. Work, date night, a wedding, the gym and more, from clothes you own.
  • Dressed for the weather. Suggestions know today's forecast for your city.
  • Your calendar, if you like. It can spot today's wedding or client meeting. Your events never leave your phone.
  • Packing lists built for you. From your wardrobe, against the forecast where you're going.

Fewer things bought twice

  • See everything you own. Search and filter across 21 categories, from shoes and bags to watches and jewellery.
  • Gaps, named. It shows which piece your wardrobe is missing, so you shop for one thing instead of a bag of maybes.
  • Check before you buy. Share a product page from Safari and see it judged against what's already in your closet.
  • Colours that suit you. An optional colour profile puts the palettes that flatter you first.

Clothes that last longer

  • Read the care label for you. Scan it and the wash advice follows the fabric.
  • Stain help. What to do about a stain before it sets.
  • Cleaners and tailors nearby. Find them, and keep track of what's been dropped off.
  • Loads that won't ruin things. Laundry keeps the reds away from the whites.

Also on your iPhone: home-screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, Live Activities for trips and dry-cleaning pickups, and an optional Face ID lock.

Your wardrobe stays on your phone

There's no ClosetMinded server to send it to. We built it that way on purpose.

  • No account to create
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
  • Moves to a new iPhone with your normal backup
  • Erase everything from Settings in one step
